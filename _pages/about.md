### 个人简介 | About Me

**刘宇阳**，北京大学信息工程学院-博雅博士后，合作导师为**田永鸿教授**（国家杰出青年）。她于2017年至2024年在中国科学院沈阳自动化研究所机器人学国家重点实验室获得博士学位，师从**丛杨教授**（国家杰出青年）。2022年至2023年，她在西班牙巴塞罗那自治大学担任访问博士生，导师为**Joost教授**。她的研究主要集中在**计算机视觉、持续学习和AI4S**等领域。
她荣获了**2024年度博士后创新人才支持计划-A类**。她先后主持了博新A计划、博士后面上项目，并参与了国家重点研发计划项目、国家自然科学基金重点项目以及多项中科院课题。
她获得的荣誉包括**国家奖学金**、**国家留学基金委奖学金**、**中国科学院大学学业一等奖学金**、中国科学院大学“三好学生”、**全国大学生电子设计大赛国家一等奖**以及**JCAR杯机器人工业应用高校邀请赛第二名**。
她已在国内外期刊和会议上发表了**20余篇文章**。代表性论文包括视觉和模式识别顶级国际会议**ICCV**论文，以及**IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (TIP)** 等IEEE Transaction系列期刊论文。目前，她担任TCSVT、CAA期刊和NeurIPS、ICML、ICLR、AAAI、ICCV会议的审稿人。

---

**Yuyang Liu** is a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Information Engineering, Peking University. Her postdoctoral co-supervisor is **Professor Yonghong Tian** (National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars). She received her Ph.D. from 2017 to 2024 at the State Key Laboratory of Robotics, Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, supervised by **Professor Yang Cong** (National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars). From 2022 to 2023, she was a visiting Ph.D. student at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, advised by **Professor Joost van de Weijer**. Her research primarily focuses on **computer vision, continual learning, and AI4S**.
She was awarded the **2024 Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program (Category A)**. She has led projects such as the Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program (Category A) and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation General Program, and participated in multiple initiatives including the National Key R&D Program, key projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and Chinese Academy of Sciences research projects.
Her accolades include the **National Scholarship**, **China Scholarship Council (CSC) Scholarship**, **First-Class Academic Scholarship from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences**, the "Triple-A Student" honor from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, **First Prize in the National College Student Electronic Design Competition**, and **Second Place in the JCAR Cup Robot Industrial Application University Invitational Competition**.
She has published **20 articles** in domestic and international journals and conferences. Her representative works include a paper at **ICCV** (a top-tier international conference in computer vision and pattern recognition), and papers in **IEEE Transactions** series journals such as **IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (TIP)**. Currently, she serves as a reviewer for journals like TCSVT, CAA, and conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, and ICCV.
