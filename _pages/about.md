### 个人简介 | About Me

我主要从事**机器学习** (持续学习、高效参数微调、分布外学习)、**计算机视觉** (2D & 3D 目标识别和检测任务) 以及 **AI4S** 等研究。目前，我担任 TPAMI、TRO、TMM、TCSVT、TVCJ、TCDS 等 IEEE Transaction 系列期刊和 CVPR、ICCV、ICLR、NeurIPS、ICML、AAAI、ECCV 等国际知名会议的审稿人。

My research interests primarily focus on **Machine Learning** (Continual Learning, Parameter Efficient Fine-tuning, Out-of-Distribution Learning), **Computer Vision** (2D & 3D Object Recognition and Detection tasks), and **AI4S**. I currently serve as a reviewer for prestigious international conferences such as CVPR, ICCV, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, ECCV, and IEEE Transaction journals including TPAMI, TRO, TMM, TCSVT, TVCJ, TCDS.
