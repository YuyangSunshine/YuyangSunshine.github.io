About Me

I am currently a Distinguished Associate Researcher at Peking University (since Jul. 2026). From Jul. 2024 to Jun. 2026, I was a Boya Postdoctoral Researcher at Peking University, working with Prof. Yonghong Tian.

I received my Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (State Key Laboratory of Robotics) in Jun. 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Yang Cong. During Sep. 2022 - Sep. 2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student supported by CSC at Computer Vision Center (CVC), Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, working with Prof. Joost van de Weijer.

My research focuses on building adaptive AI systems for scientific discovery, spanning continual learning, scientific agents and multi-agent systems, and embodied intelligence for autonomous experimentation.

Platform Demo

AI4S LAB: A One-Stop Digital Intelligent Life Science Research Platform

AI4S LAB integrates computing, data, models, and automated experiments, supporting a closed research loop from computational prediction and protocol design to robotic execution and data analysis.

  • 15+ professional datasets and 22 sets of intelligent experimental equipment
  • Natural-language-driven experimental design and execution
  • Integrated dry-lab / wet-lab workflow for life-science research
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025–2026
Project
arXiv

LabEvolver: Training-Free Experience Evolution for Safe and Grounded Wet-Lab Agents

A training-free framework that lets robotic wet-lab agents improve from grounded execution experience, coupling state-aware closed-loop operation with reusable skill, strategy, and safety experience.

  • State-grounded inner loop for perception, planning, execution, and safety validation
  • Outer loop distills completed trajectories into reusable experience without model-weight updates
  • Validated on real robotic solution-preparation tasks and continual embodied benchmarks
Jingya Wang, Yuyang Gao, Liuzhenghao Lv, Yuyang Liu · 2026
arXiv Project
NeurIPS 2026

RipplePLM: Structural and Property Decoupling for Protein Mutation Effect Generation

A mutation-aware protein language framework that separates local structural perturbation, distal contact-mediated effects, and biochemical-property signals for mutation effect generation.

  • Direct-Distal Cross-Attention separates local and multi-hop structural evidence
  • Property Latent Chain injects expert-guided biochemical supervision into latent states
  • Accepted to NeurIPS 2026
Liuzhenghao Lv, Yuyang Liu†, Yuyang Gao, Li Yuan, Yonghong Tian† · 2026
