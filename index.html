About Me

I am currently an Associate Researcher at the AI for Science Institute, Peking University (since Jul. 2026), working with Prof. Yonghong Tian (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar). Previously, I was a Boya Postdoctoral Researcher at Peking University.

I received my Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (State Key Laboratory of Robotics) in June 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Yang Cong (recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars). During Sep. 2022 - Sep. 2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student (supported by CSC) at National Center for Computer Vision Research (CVC), Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, Spain, working with Prof. Joost van de Weijer (Top 2% AI researchers in 2021).

Workshop

BioProAgent: Neuro-Symbolic Grounding for Constrained Scientific Planning

A neuro-symbolic framework that anchors LLM planning in deterministic Finite State Machines (FSM) for reliable wet-lab automation. Achieves 95.6% physical compliance for irreversible scientific experiments.

  • State-Augmented Planning: Design-Verify-Rectify workflow ensuring hardware compliance
  • Semantic Symbol Grounding: 6x token reduction through symbolic abstraction
  • 95.6% physical compliance vs 21.0% for ReAct on BioProBench
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2026

AI4S LAB: The World's First "One-Stop" Digital Intelligent Life Science Research Platform

AI4S LAB deeply integrates computing power, data, models, and experiments. The platform achieves a closed-loop process: "theoretical prediction → experimental design → automated execution → data analysis".

  • 15+ professional datasets & 22 sets of high-throughput equipment
  • 30x efficiency improvement over traditional methods
  • Natural language dialogue for AI-driven experimental design
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025

BioProBench: Comprehensive Benchmark for Biological Protocol Understanding

We introduce BioProBench – the first large-scale, multi-task benchmark for biological protocol understanding & reasoning, accelerating LLMs that truly understand lab procedures.

  • 27K real-world protocols
  • 556K instances across 5 core tasks
  • Protocol QA, Step Ordering, Error Correction, Generation & Reasoning
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025

Continual Learning for Vision-Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey

The first systematic review of continual learning in Vision-Language Models, addressing how VLMs can learn efficiently without catastrophic forgetting while remaining parameter-efficient.

  • Three Core Challenges identified
  • Novel challenge-driven taxonomy proposed
  • Comprehensive analysis of protocols & metrics
