About Me

I am currently a Boya Postdoctoral Researcher at Peking University, (since Jul. 2024), working with Prof. Yonghong Tian (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar).

I received my Ph.D. degree from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (State Key Laboratory of Robotics) (Jun. 2024), advised by Prof. Yang Cong (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar). During Sep. 2022 - Sep. 2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student (supported by CSC) at Computer Vision Center (CVC), Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, Spain, working with Prof. Joost van de Weijer (Top 2% AI researchers in 2021).

Platform Demo

AI4S LAB: The World's First "One-Stop" Digital Intelligent Life Science Research Platform

As the world's first "one-stop" digital and intelligent life science research platform, AI4S LAB deeply integrates four key elements: computing power, data, models, and experiments. Relying on the AI4S native multi-agent system BIOMA, independently developed by Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School, the platform achieves a closed-loop process encompassing "theoretical prediction → experimental design → automated execution → data analysis".

Yuyang Liu, et al. 2025

Preprint

BioProBench: Comprehensive Benchmark for Biological Protocol Understanding and Reasoning

We introduce BioProBench – the first large-scale, multi-task benchmark for biological protocol understanding & reasoning. This benchmark accelerates development of LLMs that truly understand lab procedures, improves reproducibility & safety in automated workflows, and guides researchers toward robust AI-driven experimental design.

  • 27K real-world protocols
  • 556K structured instances across 5 core tasks
  • Protocol QA, Step Ordering, Error Correction, Generation & Reasoning
Yuyang Liu, et al. 2025

Survey

Continual Learning for Vision-Language Models: A Survey and Taxonomy Beyond Forgetting

As Vision-Language Models (VLMs) achieve impressive success on multimodal tasks, a key challenge remains: How can they learn continually without catastrophically forgetting prior knowledge? We offer the first focused and systematic review of the VLM-CL field, serving as a comprehensive diagnostic reference for developing lifelong vision-language systems.

  • Three Core Challenges: Cross-Modal Feature Drift, Shared Module Interference, Zero-Shot Erosion
  • Novel Taxonomy: Multi-Modal Replay, Cross-Modal Regularization, Parameter-Efficient Adaptation
  • Comprehensive analysis of evaluation protocols, datasets, and metrics
Yuyang Liu, et al. 2025
