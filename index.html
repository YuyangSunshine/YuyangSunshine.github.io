About Me

I am currently a Boya Postdoctoral Researcher at Peking University, (since Jul. 2024), working with Prof. Yonghong Tian (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar).

I received my Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (State Key Laboratory of Robotics) in June 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Yang Cong (recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars). During Sep. 2022 - Sep. 2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student (supported by CSC) at National Center for Computer Vision Research (CVC) (CVC), Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, Spain, working with Prof. Joost van de Weijer (Top 2% AI researchers in 2021).

Platform Demo

AI4S LAB: The World's First "One-Stop" Digital Intelligent Life Science Research Platform

AI4S LAB deeply integrates computing power, data, models, and experiments. The platform achieves a closed-loop process: "theoretical prediction → experimental design → automated execution → data analysis".

  • 15+ professional datasets & 22 sets of high-throughput equipment
  • 30x efficiency improvement over traditional methods
  • Natural language dialogue for AI-driven experimental design
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025

Preprint

BioProBench: Comprehensive Benchmark for Biological Protocol Understanding

We introduce BioProBench – the first large-scale, multi-task benchmark for biological protocol understanding & reasoning, accelerating LLMs that truly understand lab procedures.

  • 27K real-world protocols
  • 556K instances across 5 core tasks
  • Protocol QA, Step Ordering, Error Correction, Generation & Reasoning
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025

Survey

Continual Learning for Vision-Language Models: A Survey and Taxonomy

How can VLMs learn continually without catastrophically forgetting prior knowledge? We offer the first systematic review of the VLM-CL field for developing lifelong vision-language systems.

  • Three Core Challenges identified
  • Novel challenge-driven taxonomy proposed
  • Comprehensive analysis of protocols & metrics
Yuyang Liu, et al. · 2025
