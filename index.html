Curriculum Vitae

About Me

I am currently a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at Peking University, working with Prof. Yonghong Tian (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar).

I received my Ph.D. degree from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (State Key Laboratory of Robotics) in 2024, advised by Prof. Yang Cong (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar). During 2022-2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at Computer Vision Center (CVC), Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, Spain, working with Prof. Joost van de Weijer (Top 2% AI researchers in 2021).
